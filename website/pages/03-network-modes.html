Network modes

Three traffic paths are independent of each other and of the Service Gateway. Enable the ones you need.

Subnet router: Tailnet → LAN

Advertise the uplink LAN and/or the ESP32's AP subnet. Turn on source NAT when LAN hosts have no route back to the tailnet. 4via6 maps an overlapping IPv4 LAN into a site-specific IPv6 prefix. Guide: Subnet router.

AP gateway for IoT

The AP can stay always on, or run recovery-only: in standby while the uplink works and back after an outage. Firewall chains and AP-side port forwarding apply to its subnet.

Exit node

As a client, the ESP32 sends AP traffic through a chosen Tailnet exit node and fails closed when it is unreachable. As a server, it advertises itself as an IPv4 exit node (approval required); IPv6 fails closed. The two modes cannot be active together. Guide: Exit node.

Ethernet

A separate esp32-s3-eth build adds an optional W5500 SPI Ethernet uplink with explicit pins, DHCP or static IPv4 and WiFi-first or Ethernet-first selection. It is host-tested but not hardware-validated; see Hardware.
