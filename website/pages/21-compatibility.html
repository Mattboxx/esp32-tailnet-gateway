Compatibility

Hardware

ItemRequirement
ChipESP32-S3 (other ESP32 families are not supported)
MemoryAt least 8 MB quad flash and 8 MB octal PSRAM: N8R8 and N16R8 modules (N16R8 tested). Not 4 MB flash, R2 or no PSRAM, or octal-flash modules — see Hardware
Wi-Fi2.4 GHz network, WPA2/WPA3 Personal or WPA2-Enterprise (PEAP/TTLS)
USBNative USB or a USB-to-UART chip, with a data cable
EthernetOptional W5500 SPI module, driver in the standard image, off until configured; experimental, not hardware-qualified

Tailnet

Tailscale (hosted) and Headscale 0.26 or newer. This is a community firmware, not an official Tailscale client; features follow the documented subset.

Installing

Browser installer: desktop Chrome or Edge (Web Serial). Easy Install package: Windows 10/11, macOS and Linux with Python 3 and esptool 5.

Known limits

  • The Web UI uses HTTP: administer it from a trusted LAN or over the tailnet.
  • The first-boot setup page and captive portal are in English; the dashboard also offers Italian, Spanish and Simplified Chinese.
  • Throughput is limited by a single 2.4 GHz radio and the userspace WireGuard stack.
  • Opening the setup page automatically depends on the phone or PC; the address http://192.168.4.1/ always works.
