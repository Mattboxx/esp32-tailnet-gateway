Example: MQTT between two homes

A controller at a second site can connect to the ESP32's LAN address and send messages to an MQTT broker on your Tailnet. The ESP32 forwards only the service covered by the rule. The controller keeps its existing network connection.

Set up your gateway

  1. Install. Use the browser installer on a computer with a USB data cable, or choose another download.
  2. Connect. Follow the first-boot wizard to pair with the board, test the uplink and find its LAN address.
  3. Choose a role. Join Tailscale or Headscale and turn on the capabilities you need.

Already running the firmware? See updates and rollback. Choosing a role? Explore all firmware features.

Before you deploy

This community firmware runs on a microcontroller. It suits control traffic, automation, dashboards and SSH. Check hardware compatibility and read the security model for the role you plan to use.
