The real firmware Web UI

These screenshots were captured from Firmware v2.0.0 running on an ESP32-S3. They are not design mockups or AI-generated images. Only the relevant panels are shown: addresses, peer names, credentials and browser chrome are outside the capture.

The same built-in interface works in a desktop or mobile browser. English is shown here; Italian, Spanish and Simplified Chinese are also available. No separate administration app is required.
