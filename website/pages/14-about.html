About ESP32 Tailnet Gateway

ESP32 Tailnet Gateway — Mattboxx Edition (formerly ESP32 Homelab Gateway) is an open-source firmware that turns one ESP32-S3 into a set of selectable Tailnet roles. Its focus is the Selective LAN → Tailnet Service Gateway, with remote recovery and diagnostics as the second pillar.
